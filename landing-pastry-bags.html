Frequently asked questions

Do you deliver to Miami and South Florida?

Yes. We serve bakeries, cafés, caterers and food trucks across Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach counties. Get in touch with your order details and we’ll confirm delivery timelines.

What custom branding options are available?

Our kraft pastry bags are available with 1-colour or 3-colour custom branded printing. Upload your logo through the order form above and our team will follow up with your print options and a quote.

Can I order in bulk?

Yes. We offer tiered bulk pricing – the more you order, the lower your unit cost. Share your estimated monthly volume in the order form and we’ll put together pricing for your business.

What else can this bag be used for besides pastries?

Its 6.5" x 1" x 8" flat-bottom footprint works well beyond pastries – think sandwiches, cookies, bread rolls, retail snacks and grab-and-go merchandise.

How long does production and delivery take?

Lead times depend on order size and whether custom printing is required. Once you submit an order request, your account manager will confirm production time and delivery to your Miami or South Florida location.

Need Something More?

Pastry bags are just the start — we offer a full range of custom takeout packaging, from cups and napkins to containers and everything in between. Send us a message and our team will get back to you shortly.
