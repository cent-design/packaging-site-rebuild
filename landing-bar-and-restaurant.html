Frequently asked questions

Do you deliver to Miami and South Florida?

Yes. We serve pizzerias, bakeries, caterers and food trucks across Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach counties. Get in touch with your order details and we’ll confirm delivery timelines.

What custom branding options are available?

Our kraft pizza boxes are available with 1-colour or 3-colour custom branded printing. Upload your logo through the order form above and our team will follow up with your print options and a quote.

Can I order in bulk?

Yes. We offer tiered bulk pricing – the more you order, the lower your unit cost. Share your estimated monthly volume in the order form and we’ll put together pricing for your business.

What else can this box be used for besides pizza?

Its 12" x 12" x 2.25" footprint works well beyond pizza – think personal pies, flatbreads, giant cookies, dessert platters, charcuterie boards, pastry spreads and retail gift packaging.

How long does production and delivery take?

Lead times depend on order size and whether custom printing is required. Once you submit an order request, your account manager will confirm production time and delivery to your Miami or South Florida location.
