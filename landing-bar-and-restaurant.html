Frequently asked questions

Which divisions of the Bar and Restaurant Industry do you cater to?

We assist a wide range of leading partners from restaurants and bars to bistros and event caterers. If you need high-performance packaging to handle a large amount of food and drink, we’d love to assist you. Feel free to visit our online shop or contact us.

Do you have sustainable food packaging options?

Yes, we do. Choose from a growing range of sustainable materials, including post-consumer rPET, FSC paper, compostable and biodegradable options to achieve environmental goals and business performance.

Can I order Bar and Restaurant packaging in bulk or for multiple branches?

Yes, you can. You can place a multi-branch or bulk order using Takeout Goods Direct or by contacting your Takeout Goods Account Manager.

How long will it take to receive my food packaging order?

Lead times depend on the type of order. Production for custom-designed products is completed within four weeks from the date of your approved order. Your Account Manager will let you know when to expect delivery to your final destination. Standard, in-stock products will have a short delivery time of 1-3 business days locally.

Can I track the progress of my order?

Yes, we have a dedicated support team to update you on the progress of your order from production to delivery.
